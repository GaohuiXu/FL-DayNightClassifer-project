Why bb02d sits ~0.12 mAP below BEVFusion-base (our reference, not the SOTA)

The ~0.12 mAP gap (0.5656 → 0.685), decomposed. The dominant piece is voxel resolution (0.2 m vs 0.075 m), which is coupled to the no-spconv constraint (Rule #2) — sparse 3D conv makes 0.075 m affordable; our dense 2D grid can't. The pure structural-encoding penalty is small (~0.02–0.03).

bb02d (ours) 0.5656 voxel resolution 0.2→0.075 m (≈0.055) recipe CBGS/GT-paste (≈0.030, not transferable) structural 3D-sparse vs 2D-dense (≈0.025)

Reading it: ≈0.055 (the largest) is voxel resolution — fully closing it would need a 0.075 m grid, infeasible for our dense 2D BEV (1365² cells) but cheap for BEVFusion's sparse 3D conv → this gap is the documented portability cost of Rule #2. ≈0.030 is the recipe (CBGS + GT-paste) — we ran all three this session and they were net-negative on our already-strong LiDAR stack, so it is not closeable for us. ≈0.025 is the pure structural penalty (2D-dense vs 3D-sparse encoding), which at matched resolution is small (dense PillarNet 0.599 ≈ sparse CenterPoint-SECOND 0.596).
